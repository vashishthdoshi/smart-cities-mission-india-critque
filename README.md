# Smart Cities Mission - A Policy Critque

## The impact of the Smart Cities Mission in India and its persisting implementation issues

### Policy Brief
This is an executive brief of the policy analysis, aimed at managerial audiences who want a quick overview of the implementation problems the Smart Cities Mission faced.

### Policy Memo
This is a detailed evidence based memo that outlines core challenges that ail the Smart Cities Mission and proposes 2 tested and actionable policy interventions that address these challenges. This is aimed for in-depth understanding of the policy issue and recommends actionable insight.

### Policy Blog
This is aimed at a generalist audience and for web-based interactive interfaces. This explains the smart cities missions challenges and implementation gaps in an informal manner for mass consumption

### Note:
This was an academic exercise and I have posed as a researcher at the fictional "Heinz Policy Institute". This was meant to practise in almost-real environments. Every agency, personnel and subject-matter otherwise mentioned is completely based on real-world facts an figures.
